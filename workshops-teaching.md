## Workshops and Teaching ##

The times when I am most useful, and people actually learn things by doing. 

---
"Making Names Visible: Hands-on Wikidata Training," Association of Tribal Archives, Libraries, and Museums (ATALM) Annual Conference, Spokane, WA, September 2026. Workshop developed and presented with Diana Marsh, Ugoma Smoke, and Amanda Sorensen for the Transforming Indigenous Archival Search (TIAS) project, introducing linked data and Wikidata reconciliation practices for cultural-group and archival entity description.

"Developing an AI-assisted Python Workflow for an Archive of Native American-produced Films," CAFe + L4CCH Vibe Coding Workshop, University of Maryland, August 18, 2026. Demonstration of a Python workflow developed with ChatGPT to normalize and disambiguate asset IDs for Vision Maker Media's archival collections.

"OpenRefine + Wikidata Workshop," University of Maryland College of Information, August 2026. Developed and taught a two-part workshop for the Transforming Indigenous Archival Search (TIAS) project team, using Bureau of Indian Affairs tribal-name data to introduce data cleaning, reconciliation, authority matching, and quality control.

[CINE 419E: Digital Storytelling with Archives](https://app.testudo.umd.edu/soc/202201/CINE/CINE419E). University of Maryland undergraduate course, Cinema and Media Studies, Spring 2022, Spring 2023, Spring 2024, and Spring 2026. Incorporates creative research and storytelling using archival media, including hands-on audiovisual preservation and digitization activities in MITH's Memory Lab.

"CAFe + MITH + SNAC OpenRefine Wikidata Plugin Workshop," University of Maryland, November 2025. Developed and taught a workshop on archival metadata cleaning and linked-data reconciliation using film catalog data from Greenbelt Cinema's 16mm collection.

"Archival Research Methods for Documentary Filmmaking," Guest lecture for JOUR779Z: Multimodal Research Methods, University of Maryland Philip Merrill College of Journalism, April 2025. Introduced graduate documentary filmmaking students to locating, evaluating, and contextualizing archival material.

"Platforms and Practices for Activating Community Knowledge," Association for Information Science and Technology (ASIS&T) Virtual Research Symposium. Co-presented with Maxine Gross on the Lakeland Digital Archive, March 2023.

["Collectors’ Knowledge – The Sound Submissions Project of the Radio Preservation Task Force](https://www.arsc-audio.org/pdf/ARSC_webinar_20220728_CollectorsKnowledge_flyer.pdf)," Association of Recorded Sound Collections (ARSC) Continuing Education Webinar, July 2022.
